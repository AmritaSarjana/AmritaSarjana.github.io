Founder lessons

What running two companies taught me, in pictures.

Real trade-offs, and a few hard lessons from building Truzell and Daily Status.

Truzell · unit economics

A ₹1,000 order looked good on top, but cost me money.

Once I added operations, payments and support, each order ended up in the red.

Sale price+₹1,000
Commission (10%)−₹100
Inspection−₹40
Pickup & delivery−₹120
Payment gateway (2%)−₹20
Support & disputes−₹40
Customer acquisition−₹750
Net per order: −₹70

Good demand, but the unit economics didn't work.

Truzell · operations

Every order created work beyond the product.

The real challenge wasn't building the marketplace. It was running the operations behind it.

  1. Inspect
  2. Pickup
  3. Deliver
  4. Support
  5. Resolve

Lesson: The product wasn't the marketplace. The operation was.

Daily Status · growth & spend

Growth can look healthy while economics are still fragile.

What each ₹1 of ad spend brought back, by month of sign-up.

Lesson: Scale changes the maths. Early cohorts paid back ~2×, and new cohorts need renewals to turn a profit.

Daily Status · the funnel

Out of every 100 installs, ~13 took a trial.

~13 took a trial~87 didn't

100K+ installs and 20K+ transactions.

Lesson: Get more people to start a trial, then focus on converting them to paid.
