    Founder lessons

    What running two companies taught me, in pictures.

    These are the numbers I lived with every morning. Truzell figures are illustrative; Daily Status figures are a 30-day snapshot.

    Truzell · one fridge order

    I earned ₹960. I kept the price of a samosa and chai.

    Commission on an ₹8,000 fridge+₹960
    Inspection−₹180
    Pickup and delivery−₹350
    Payment gateway−₹60
    Support and disputes−₹70
    Finding the customer−₹250
    🥟☕
    ₹50 left per order

    Trust doesn't scale like software. Every guarantee needed a person.

    Lesson: I went looking for a market where the product itself does the work. That became Daily Status.

    Daily Status · paywall v3

    Out of every 100 people on a free trial, 8 more started paying.

    8 extra paying users after v3The other 92 trial users

    Shipped on day 8; the jump held. Found with Mixpanel funnels, decided in a spreadsheet.

    Lesson: one screen moved 8 people in every 100. Small tests, big levers.

    Daily Status · ad campaigns

    For every ₹1 I spent, here's what came back.

    Each install cost ₹11.5, about the price of a cutting chai.

    Lesson: speak the user's language, literally. Odia video got almost ₹2 back for every ₹1, so it scaled. Tamil static got 60 paise, so it was paused.

    Daily Status · the see-saw

    A paying user is worth 2.5× what it costs to find them.

    Lesson: LTV : CAC of 2.5×, paid back in about 4 months. The heavier side wins.
