Chapter 02 · Lead · IQVIA, Technical lead

Leading at scale

Modernised a translation platform used by 1M+ people, taking millions of requests a day and documents up to 150 MB.

1M+Users
~70%Better scalability
60%Faster deploys
8Engineers led
Problem
A monolith that couldn't scale with document volume, and translation quality that depended on one engine.
What I built
Re-architected the monolith into microservices on Kubernetes and Azure. Built a multi-engine translation layer routing across OpenAI, Google, DeepL, Microsoft and in-house models. Architected OCR, document processing, real-time tracking and audit workflows.
What moved
~50% higher accuracy · ~50% less processing time
Carried forward
Leading eight engineers taught me to align people, unblock teams and make decisions others can build on. That's the job of a founder's office.

Chapter 03 · Build · Truzell, Founder

Making strangers trust each other

People needed to sell quickly. Buyers wanted a good deal. The problem? Neither trusted the stranger on the other side. Truzell sat in the middle and made the transaction feel safe.

10K+Downloads
20+Orders delivered
iOS + AndroidAvailable on both
The user
Someone switching jobs, moving to a new city or country, or heading back home — with days to empty a flat.
The bet
Buyers wanted certainty. Sellers wanted speed. Truzell had to deliver both.
What I built
Built and shipped the product end to end across iOS and Android, then owned growth through Meta Ads, SEO and referrals.
